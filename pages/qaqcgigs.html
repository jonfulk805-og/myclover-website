❓ FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need my own hardware?

Yes — you'll need a machine capable of running CloverOS (bare metal or VM). Most modern x86_64 hardware with 4GB+ RAM works. VPS testing environments may be provided for some gigs.

How often are release cycles?

CloverOS targets regular release cycles. Each release opens a new round of gig slots. Active contractors get early notice. Expect multiple opportunities per quarter.

What's CloverCoin and why the bonus?

CloverCoin (CC) is MyClover.Tech's XRPL-native digital currency. 100 CC = $1. Choosing CC payment earns you a 10-25% bonus and gives you spending power across the entire MCT ecosystem — marketplace, apps, training, hardware.

Can I stack multiple gigs?

Absolutely. If you have the skills and bandwidth, claim Functional QA + Security + Performance in one cycle. Each gig pays independently. More work = more money.

What happens to my QA LLM?

Your AI assistant's knowledge from testing gets preserved, cleaned, and deployed as a support bot on that release. You get credit. EHBB Elite contractors get a named bot.

Is this a W-2 job?

No. All QA/QC gigs are freelance, self-employed contracts. You're an independent contractor. Invoice for completed work, get paid directly. Handle your own taxes.
