Cost Basis Methods — You Choose

Select per asset: FIFO (First In, First Out), LIFO (Last In, First Out), HIFO (Highest In, First Out), or Specific Identification. CloverTax calculates the optimal method and shows you the difference — Chappie will tell you which one saves the most.

FIFO
First In, First Out
IRS default. Sells oldest lots first.
HIFO
Highest In, First Out
Minimizes gains. Chappie's pick for most users.
LIFO
Last In, First Out
Sells newest lots first. Good in rising markets.
SPEC ID
Specific Identification
Cherry-pick lots. Maximum control.
