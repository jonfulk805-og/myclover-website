    Field manual

    How to stay a ghost

    The job

    • Kill every target before they leave. Use the binoculars [F] to tag people. Tagged people show through walls, and tagging a target tells you who it is.
    • Targets keep a schedule: they go indoors, come out on balconies, smoke by the pool. Watch first, then shoot.
    • If the alarm goes off, the targets run for the helicopter. If one gets aboard, the contract fails.

    Staying hidden

    • The visibility bar shows how easy you are to see. Lie prone [Z] in a bush and stay still. Moving, standing and searchlights all make you easy to see.
    • The arcs around your crosshair show who is noticing you. White is curious, amber is suspicious, red means you have been seen.
    • Guards who find a body, or see someone drop, start a search. Shoot people where nobody is watching.
    • At dusk, looking through the scope toward the sun puts a glint on your lens, and people can see it.

    The shot

    • Supersonic rounds fly flat and fast but everyone hears them. Fire one when the sound is masked (the generator coughs, a plane passes, thunder rolls) and nobody notices. Three unmasked shots and they find you.
    • Subsonic rounds [B] are almost silent but slow. They drop a lot and need more lead on walkers.
    • The rifle is zeroed at 100 m. Past that, hold high using the mil dots, and allow for the wind arrow. Moving targets need lead.
    • Hold Shift while scoped to hold your breath and steady the sway. On Marksman this also shows the red impact diamond. Recruit always shows it. Ghost never does.
    • Headshots and body shots kill. A limb hit makes them scream, and that starts the alarm. Red barrels and the fuel tanks explode, and an explosion looks like an accident.

    Controls

    • WASD move, Mouse aim, LMB fire, RMB scope, Wheel zoom, Shift hold breath / sprint
    • C crouch, Z prone, F binoculars, R reload, B switch ammo, N night vision, Tab map, Esc pause, M mute
    • Gamepad: sticks, LT scope, RT fire, RB hold breath, Y binoculars, B stance, X reload, LB ammo, D-pad zoom, Back map
