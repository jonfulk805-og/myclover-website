VOID CORSAIR
Hold the gun deck. Gut the pirate fleet. Steal their scrap.
Original prototype by Grok Game Studio · v2 by Viktor for The Clover Arcade

HOW TO PLAY

Mouse: aim · hold left click to fire · hold right click or Space to shield.
Move: WASD / arrows · Dash: Shift (or Q / E for left / right).
Weapons: 1–4 or mouse wheel · R vents heat · P / Esc pause · M mute.
Gamepad: left stick move, right stick aim, RT fire, LT shield, A dash, LB/RB weapons, Start pause.
Touch: left stick moves, drag the right side to aim and fire, SHIELD and DASH buttons.

Parry: raise the shield just before a bolt hits it and the bolt flies back at the shooter.
Close calls: dodging a bolt by a hair scores points and triggers brief bullet-time.
Boarders (red, spiked) rush the deck and explode. Kill them first.
Snipers paint you with a laser before firing. Move.
Scrap: every kill drops scrap. Spend it between waves on weapons, hull, shields and thrusters.
Every 5th wave: the Black Flag Dreadnought. Break its 3 turrets to drop the core shield, then gut the core.
