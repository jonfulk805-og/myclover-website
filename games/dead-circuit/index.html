How to play

Mission

  • Clear each sector by destroying every robot and every machine stronghold (the boarded houses with green-lit windows and antenna masts). Clear all three to win.
  • Robots take 3 hits. Strongholds take 6 hits.
  • Enemies fire green plasma. Their weapon glows before it fires, so watch for the charge and get out of the way.
  • Every 4th kill restores 8 hull. Clearing a sector restores 15 hull.
  • Quick kills build a score multiplier up to x5. Taking a hit resets it.

Controls
