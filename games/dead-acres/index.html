Field manual

How to survive

The basics

  • They come out of the corn and tear the boards off the windows. Hold E at a window to nail boards back on (+10 each).
  • Every hit is +10. A kill is +60, a headshot kill +100, a knife kill +130.
  • Health comes back if you avoid getting hit for a few seconds. Three hits and you're down (two on Hard).
  • Each round brings more of them, and they're tougher. From about round 4 some of them run.

Spending points

  • Wall guns: buy the guns hanging on the walls. Buy again for ammo at half price.
  • Doors: open the Yard, the Barn and the Tool Shed. More room to run, more windows to watch.
  • Mystery box (950): a random weapon, maybe the Clover Ray. Too many rolls and it moves somewhere else.
  • Power: the switch is in the Tool Shed. It powers the perk machines and the Clover Forge in the barn (5000 upgrades your gun).

Perks

  • Second Wind 500. You get back up once. Works without power.
  • Iron Hide 2500. +150 health, roughly double the hits to go down.
  • Quick Draw 3000. Reloads twice as fast.
  • Double Tap 2000. Fires faster and hits harder.
  • Stamina Leaf 2000. Sprint faster and for longer.

Power-ups (glowing green)

  • Max Ammo, Insta-Kill, Double Points, Kaboom (kills everything on screen) and Carpenter (fixes every window).

Controls

  • WASD move, Shift sprint, Mouse aim, LMB fire, RMB aim down sights
  • R reload, E use / buy, V or F knife, G grenade, Q / wheel / 12 switch guns, Esc pause, M mute
  • Gamepad: sticks, RT fire, LT aim, X use / reload, Y swap, RB grenade, B knife, L3 sprint
