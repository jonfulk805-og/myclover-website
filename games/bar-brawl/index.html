HOW TO BRAWL

Move

WASD walk, Shift stagger faster, Space hop, mouse to look.

Fists

LMB left, RMB right. Tap to jab, hold and release for a haymaker. Alternate fists to build a combo.

Grab anything

E picks up stools, chairs, bottles, cues, kegs... and dazed or knocked-down people. Holding something? LMB swings it, RMB (hold) throws it. G drops it.

Defend

Q raises your guard. Block just as a red fist lands to PARRY and stun them. F front-kicks people into furniture.

Liquid courage

E on a full drink (or at the taps) to chug it: heals your grit and hits harder. Too much BUZZ and you black out.

Run up the tab

Smash tables, mirrors, shelves, the jukebox. The bigger the BAR TAB, the more stars. Clear every wave, then take out the boss.
